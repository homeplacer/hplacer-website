3:T1ae8,<p>When you buy a brand-new home, one quiet worry tends to sit in the back of your mind: what happens if something breaks? The water heater quits. A breaker won&#39;t reset. You spot a crack you didn&#39;t notice at the walk-through. With an older resale home, that&#39;s usually just your problem to solve. With a new home from Home Placer, it isn&#39;t — because your home comes backed by real, written warranty coverage. Not a vague promise, not &quot;we&#39;ll see,&quot; but layered protection that follows the home for years.</p>
<p>A lot of buyers don&#39;t fully understand what that coverage includes, or how to actually use it. So let&#39;s walk through it plainly — what&#39;s covered, for how long, and exactly how you put it to work when you need it.</p>
<h2>The Three Layers of Coverage</h2>
<p>Your new home isn&#39;t protected by just one warranty. Three things work together, each doing a different job:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The full 1-year home warranty</strong> — covers the home itself through your first year of ownership.</li>
<li><strong>The 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty</strong> — a separate, nationally recognized new-home warranty that runs for up to 10 years.</li>
<li><strong>The 30-day walk-through</strong> — a built-in check-in after you&#39;ve actually lived in the home for a month.</li>
</ul>
<p>Think of them as overlapping circles. Your first year, you&#39;ve got the most coverage you&#39;ll ever have. As that year winds down, the longer-term 2-10 warranty keeps carrying the load on the things that matter most. And the 30-day walk-through is there at the very beginning to catch the small stuff before it turns into a headache.</p>
<h2>The Full 1-Year Home Warranty</h2>
<p>This is your first line of protection, and it&#39;s broad. For the first year you own the home, the home itself is under warranty. That&#39;s the period when little things tend to surface — a door that sticks once the house settles, a fixture that wasn&#39;t seated quite right, trim that needs an adjustment. Normal new-home stuff.</p>
<p>The point of the first-year warranty is simple: you shouldn&#39;t be paying out of pocket to fix defects in a home you just bought new. If it&#39;s a workmanship or materials issue rather than ordinary wear or something you damaged, it&#39;s meant to be made right. So in that first year, you don&#39;t have to wonder whether a problem is &quot;worth bothering anyone about.&quot; Report it. That&#39;s what the coverage is for.</p>
<h2>Demystifying the &quot;2-10&quot;</h2>
<p>The term <strong>2-10 Home Buyers Warranty</strong> confuses a lot of people, so let&#39;s clear it up. The &quot;2-10&quot; isn&#39;t a brand gimmick — it&#39;s the name of a long-standing national new-home warranty program, and the numbers tell you exactly what you&#39;re getting:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>2 years on systems</strong> — your home&#39;s working guts: plumbing, electrical, and HVAC (heating and cooling). The pipes, wiring, and the system that keeps you comfortable.</li>
<li><strong>10 years on major structural</strong> — the bones of the home. The load-bearing elements that hold everything up.</li>
</ul>
<p>Here&#39;s why that matters. Systems problems usually show up early if they&#39;re going to show up at all — so two years of coverage on plumbing, electrical, and HVAC catches them. Structural problems are rarer but far more serious and expensive, so those get a full decade of protection.</p>
<p>This is the same kind of nationally backed warranty that gets put on site-built homes. That&#39;s worth sitting with for a second, because it answers the biggest misconception out there.</p>
<h2>Backed Like a Site-Built Home</h2>
<p>There&#39;s an old assumption that a manufactured home is somehow flimsier or less &quot;real&quot; than a stick-built house. Today&#39;s HUD-code homes from builders like <strong>Clayton, Cavco, and Champion</strong> put that to rest — real drywall, energy-efficient construction, genuine floor plans. These are not the trailers of decades past.</p>
<p>And the warranty backs up the point. A new Home Placer home carries that same 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty you&#39;d expect on a brand-new site-built house. The structural bones get ten years. The systems get two. The home gets a full first year on top of that. When the protection is built the same way, you can stop thinking of it as a lesser kind of home — because the people standing behind it don&#39;t.</p>
<h2>How to Actually Use Your Coverage</h2>
<p>A warranty only helps if you use it, and using it is more straightforward than people expect. A few practical habits make it painless:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Keep your paperwork.</strong> When you close, you&#39;ll receive your warranty documents. Tuck them somewhere you&#39;ll find them — a drawer, a folder, a photo on your phone. They spell out what&#39;s covered and who to contact.</li>
<li><strong>Use the 30-day walk-through.</strong> About a month after move-in, you get a walk-through specifically to catch anything that&#39;s come up now that you&#39;re living in the home. Walk every room beforehand. Run the faucets, flip the switches, open and close the windows and doors. Jot down anything that seems off, even if it feels minor. This is the natural moment to flag those first-year items.</li>
<li><strong>Report issues when you notice them.</strong> Don&#39;t sit on a problem hoping it sorts itself out. The sooner you report, the easier it is to address — and the clearer it is that it&#39;s a covered defect rather than later wear.</li>
<li><strong>Know which layer applies.</strong> A sticking door in month three? First-year home warranty. An HVAC system acting up in year two? That&#39;s the 2-10 systems coverage. A structural concern years down the road? The 2-10 structural coverage runs a full ten years.</li>
<li><strong>When in doubt, ask.</strong> You don&#39;t have to diagnose it yourself or figure out which warranty owns it. If something seems wrong, reach out and let the coverage do its job.</li>
</ul>
<h2>The Short Version</h2>
<p>A new home from Home Placer is protected three ways at once: a <strong>full 1-year warranty</strong> on the home, a <strong>2-10 Home Buyers Warranty</strong> (two years on plumbing, electrical, and HVAC; ten years on the structure), and a <strong>30-day walk-through</strong> to catch the early stuff. It&#39;s the same kind of layered, nationally backed protection that stands behind a brand-new site-built home — which is exactly the point.</p>
