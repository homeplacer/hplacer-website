Frequently asked questions

Everything we get asked about buying a new home on land in Horry County. Still curious? Call us — a real person answers.
What's included in a Home Placer land-home package?+

One price covers the brand-new home, the land it sits on, delivery, professional set on a permanent foundation, tie-downs and skirting, permits, and utility hookups (power, water, and septic or sewer). It's a single closing and a single point of contact.

Can I put a home on land I already own?+

Absolutely. If you have a lot or family land, we'll evaluate the site, handle the permits and setup, and place any of our homes on it. Call us and we'll walk the property with you.

Do your homes come with land, or just the home?+

Both ways. Most buyers do a complete land-home package, but if you already have land we'll sell and set the home on your lot. Either way, we handle the setup.

How much does a new manufactured home on land cost?+

Packages start in the low $200s and vary by model, size, and lot. Because every site is different, we give you an exact all-in number for the home and land together — just ask.

Is there an HOA?+

No. You own your land outright with no homeowners association and no monthly association fees or rules board.

What brands do you carry?+

We hand-pick floor plans from Clayton, Cavco, and Champion — three of the most trusted manufactured-home builders in the country — and place them on land across Horry County.

What kind of financing is available?+

FHA, VA, and conventional loans all work for a manufactured-home-on-land purchase, often with low or no down payment. We're not a lender, but we connect you with lenders who specialize in these loans and help every credit situation.

Where do you place homes?+

Across the Grand Strand and Horry County — Myrtle Beach, Conway, Loris, Longs, and Aynor — on scattered lots, so your home isn't confined to one subdivision.

Is a manufactured home the same as a mobile home?+

Today's manufactured homes are built to a strict federal HUD code and are a world apart from the old 'mobile homes.' They're energy-efficient, durable, and come with modern finishes and warranties.

Do manufactured homes hold their value?+

When you own the land underneath, a manufactured home behaves much more like a site-built home for value. Land ownership and HUD-code construction are the keys, and that's exactly what our packages are built around.

What warranty comes with the home?+

A limited one-year warranty, plus a 30-day walk-through after you move in so anything that needs attention gets handled.

How long does the whole process take?+

From picking your home to getting keys is typically a matter of weeks, not the months a site-built home takes — we coordinate permits, delivery, foundation, and utilities for you.
